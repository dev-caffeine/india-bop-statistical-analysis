# India's Balance of Payments: A Statistical Analysis

A statistical study of India's quarterly Balance of Payments (BoP), using 25+ years of data published by the Reserve Bank of India, from Q1 2000-01 to Q1 2026-27 (315 observations, USD millions).

The analysis covers descriptive statistics, probability, distribution fitting, correlation, regression and hypothesis testing. It's applied to real macroeconomic data instead of textbook examples.

## Methods
- **Descriptive statistics:** mean, median, SD, skewness, kurtosis, IQR, coefficient of variation
- **Grouped frequency tables** and ogive
- **Outlier analysis** (1.5 × IQR rule)
- **Moments:** raw and central
- **Probability:** conditional probability, Bayes' theorem
- **Distributions:** Binomial, Poisson, Normal / Z
- **Normality testing:** Jarque–Bera
- **Correlation:** Pearson correlation matrix
- **Regression:** simple and multiple linear regression (OLS)
- **Hypothesis tests:** one-sample t-test, Welch's two-sample t-test, regression-slope t-test
